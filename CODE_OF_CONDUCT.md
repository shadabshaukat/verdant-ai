# Community conduct

Keep discussion respectful, specific and focused on the work. Welcome questions and differing experience levels, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give people room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or sensitive database information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Report a concern privately to the maintainer when possible; request a private channel without publishing another person's personal details.
